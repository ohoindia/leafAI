Providers and data flows

  • OpenAI: leaf images and analysis context are sent to the AI service to generate plant-health guidance. Provider processing and retention depend on its applicable terms and the account configuration.
  • Open-Meteo: location searches and forecast coordinates are sent to obtain weather information.
  • OpenStreetMap / Nominatim: coordinates may be sent for reverse geocoding to identify the district.
  • Government market data: location or district queries retrieve available mandi reports through data.gov.in.
  • Razorpay: web checkout and recurring mandates process payment details. The app backend verifies provider identifiers and stores subscription status; do not send card details or UPI PINs to support.
  • Hosting and infrastructure providers: configured servers, databases and upload storage process app records. [CONFIRM PRODUCTION PROVIDERS, REGIONS AND ANY OTP/SMS PROVIDERS BEFORE PUBLICATION].

Permissions and choices

Camera access is used when you take a photo. Photo selection lets you choose images. Location permission supports location-based features; a selected place or saved farm location can also be used where offered. Microphone access is requested for supported voice features. You can change device permissions in settings; the related feature may then be unavailable.

Cookies and device storage

The website uses authentication cookies and device storage for sessions and preferences. Mobile credentials use secure device storage. This policy does not assume advertising or analytics integrations are present; any such integration must be disclosed if introduced.

Security

The app includes hashed credentials, session controls and encrypted leaf-upload storage. Production deployments should use HTTPS and restricted infrastructure access. No system can guarantee absolute security. Never share passwords, login PINs, OTPs or payment PINs.
