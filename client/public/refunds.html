Refunds & cancellation

Stop future renewals, understand paid access, and get help with payment problems.

Cancel future AutoPay payments

  1. Sign in and open Subscription.
  2. Select Cancel AutoPay.
  3. Refresh the payment status and check that cancellation has been recorded.

Cancellation stops future debits on the web subscription. Access already paid for remains available until the expiry recorded in your account, including a verified introductory month. Cancel before the next scheduled debit; a charge already processed may need a separate refund request.

Uninstalling the app, signing out or simply not using it does not cancel AutoPay. If cancellation fails, contact support with your subscription reference. Store subscriptions, if offered, must also be managed through the relevant store.

When to request a refund

Contact us about duplicate charges, paid access that did not activate, debits after confirmed cancellation, or a material service failure. Include your registered contact, payment reference, date, amount and a short description. We will review the payment and access records.

Proposed refund policy - confirm before publishing: submit ordinary requests within 7 calendar days of the charge. Verified duplicate, incorrect or post-cancellation charges will be refunded. If paid service cannot be delivered or restored within a reasonable time, an appropriate full or partial refund will be considered. A change of mind or unused time after access was provided does not automatically qualify for a refund. Applicable statutory rights remain available regardless of this ordinary request window.

Review and processing timelines

Proposed support targets - confirm operational capacity: acknowledge requests within 2 business days and communicate a decision within 7 business days after receiving the necessary details. Initiate approved refunds within 3 business days of approval, normally to the original payment method. Bank/provider credit times vary and are not guaranteed by the app; we will provide available refund references and tracking information.

Introductory and annual payments

The ₹1 first month is a paid introductory offer, available once per account. It is reviewed under the same payment-error rules. Choosing Yearly authorizes a ₹999 annual charge after the introductory month. Cancellation does not automatically create a prorated refund for an annual period already paid for.

Failed or pending payments

If money was debited but access is missing, refresh the subscription status and contact support before paying again. A pending or failed payment may be reversed by the payment provider instead of requiring a merchant refund. Do not share your OTP, full card information or UPI PIN.
